SCREEN SHARING
Share this HTML file via screen share. Keep facilitator tab open in second window for reference. Toggle between tabs live as you build the narrative.
CAMERA PROTOCOL
Cameras on for opening & mirror moment. Cameras optional during data entry. Watch body language when revealing CM gap.
BREAKOUTS
Use breakouts for Portfolio tab (by function) if group >12. Reconvene to consolidate. Assign scribes to capture initiatives.
CHAT & POLLS
Use chat for async input during Portfolio build. Run quick polls: "CM <5%: Red flag?" Get consensus visible.
ENERGY BREAKS
Break every 60 mins. 10 mins max. Schedule break BEFORE CM tab so they come back fresh for the reveal.
RED FLAGS
Watch for: multitasking (emails during mirror moment), sidebar chats, early departures. Call it out kindly: "Can I get everyone back?"
Quick Reference — Your North Star
The job of this session: Get the client to see the gap between their AI ambition and their change management investment. Everything else is setup for that conversation.
• You succeed when they commit to closing the CM gap with budget and ownership
• You fail when they leave thinking "interesting tool" but take no action
• The Mirror works when someone senior says the quiet part out loud: "We've never funded this properly"
• Trust the silence after the question. Do not fill it. Let them sit in it. That's where the insight happens.
