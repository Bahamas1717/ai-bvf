Release note

What changed in v0.3.0

Until this release the diagnostic scored AI initiatives against executive KPIs alone, the numbers a CFO sees at year-end. That altitude is too high for the conversation that moves the work. v0.3.0 restores the rung that the 2010 source material always carried, named operating KPIs per industry, anchored to the executive KPI they roll into.

Two changes, both load-bearing

The operating-KPI layer is back, and the first four industry modules ship with it

The diagnostic now models three layers, executive KPI, operating KPI, initiative. Operating KPIs are optional input, so v0.2.x callers continue to work, supplying them raises the confidence band on the verdict. Retail, financial services, oil and gas, and healthcare ship today, each one carrying a named operating-KPI vocabulary, a rollup map to executive KPIs, and a worked example per verdict class.

The framework

Three layers, read top down

The BVF scores each link in the chain rather than collapsing it. The board KPI is the destination, the operating KPI is the rung that moves on a Tuesday, the initiative is the thing being funded, and the verdict tells the CFO whether to stop it, fix it, or accelerate it.

Layer 1Executive KPI

The destination, the number measured at the board review.

Revenue, gross margin, working capital, cycle time, OPEX intensity.

Layer 2Operating KPI

The rung restored in v0.3.0, industry-specific, anchored to the executive KPI it rolls into.

Same-store sales, days to invoice, refinery throughput, straight-through processing, readmission rate.

Layer 3Initiative, verdict

The thing being funded, scored against the operating KPI it claims to move.

Stop, the work is consuming budget without moving the operating KPI. Fix, the work is salvageable but mis-scoped. Accelerate, the operating KPI is moving, fund the next stage.
