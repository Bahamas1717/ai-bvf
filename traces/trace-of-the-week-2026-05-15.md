## Introductory note (one-time, top of issue)

A new section ships in this issue. Trace of the Week takes one named AI initiative from the week, runs it through the AI Value Trace, and asks the question the board did not put on the agenda. The Trace is a directed graph that maps a board outcome to the named agent that moves it, through the named human who gates it. If you want the protocol, it is open at github.com/Bahamas1717/ai-bvf.

## Trace of the Week

A 12 billion EUR European retail bank is piloting Salesforce Agentforce across 800 relationship managers. The board has signed off. The CTO is sponsor. The metric on the steering committee deck is sessions per relationship manager per week.

Current path. Board outcome, cost to income ratio. Initiative, next best action. Operating metric, adoption. Function, IT. Capability, Agentforce. Accountable human, CTO.

The Trace flags the path. Adoption is not an outcome, it is attendance. The OperatingMetric that instruments cost to income is adviser productivity, revenue per FTE, measured at 90 days. The Function is sales, not IT. The AccountabilityLayer on the gating edge has to be the CRO or the COO, not the CTO, because the CTO does not own the income line.

Recommended path. Same Agentforce rollout. New gate. CRO co-signs the productivity target before the next board cycle.

### Reframing question

If your COO cannot put a basis-point promise on cost to income next quarter, what is your CTO actually buying with Agentforce, attendance or output?
