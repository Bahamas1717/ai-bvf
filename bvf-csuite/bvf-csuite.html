Every AI initiative in your organisation lands on people. Their workflows change. Their roles evolve. Their anxiety about relevance rises. The Prosci benchmark is clear — initiatives with excellent change management are six times more likely to meet objectives. Yet most enterprises allocate less than 2% of AI project budgets to change. That gap is your problem to surface.

What the BVF gives you
A change management health assessment for every AI initiative — benchmarked against Prosci's 10% minimum standard. It reframes the conversation from "AI replaces jobs" to "AI funds reskilling."
